A selection of products, open-source libraries, and experiments I've worked on.

## At Stripe

* [Stripe Projects][stripe-projects]: Tools for developers and AI agents to provision services and manage their billing from the command line.
* [Stripe Billing][stripe-billing]: Subscription and recurring billing infrastructure. I spoke about our work refactoring its billing engine using Domain Driven Design at [BOB 2026][bob-2026].

## Side projects

* [Board][board]: A collaborative whiteboard for sketching and sharing ideas with others in real time.
* [jot away][jotaway]: A simple place for daily notes and todos.
* vindex: Semantic video search that lets you find moments in a video using natural language. I wrote about [building its processing pipeline with Temporal][vindex-writeup].
* [Supportpage.io][supportpage]: A help desk with semantic search across support articles and AI-assisted replies to support tickets.

## Earlier commercial work

* [Bahn-Buddy][bbuddy]: A service I co-founded to help rail passengers claim compensation for delays. Sold to RightNow GmbH.
* TramCloud: A company I co-founded to build tools for measuring and analyzing tram rail wear.
* [Checkpad MED][cpmed]: A mobile electronic patient record for hospital staff, which I worked on as a software engineer.
* [Factis][factis]: Software for data collection and reporting in health and social care, which I worked on as a software engineer.

## Open source

* [Spock][spock]: A lightweight Haskell web framework with type-safe routing, sessions, and database pooling.
* [SuperRecord][superrecord]: Fast anonymous records and variants for Haskell. See the [implementation write-up][superrecord-writeup] for the design and trade-offs.
* [dockercook][dockercook]: A build tool for managing Docker image layers and speeding up builds and deployment.
* [WebSocketServer][websocketserver]: A C++ WebSocket server with Lua integration.

More of my work is available as [Haskell packages on Hackage][hackage] and [repositories on GitHub][github].

## Games and experiments

* [Manager's Life][managerslife]: A multiplayer browser game I wrote in 2011. The hosted game is discontinued; its source is available on GitHub.
* [TribalWars LAN][twlan]: A version of Tribal Wars for playing on a local network.

Other early projects include a ReTi simulator and the iOS game Bean Bouncer.
